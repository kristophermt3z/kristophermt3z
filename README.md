I’m a Full-Stack Engineer who likes turning slow, manual processes into automated tools that people can run on their own.

I’ve worked across e-commerce, fintech and government, and I’ve launched my own SaaS. Along the way I’ve built AI-powered data pipelines, LLM-based evaluation systems, self-service dashboards, secure payment systems and digital wallets, and a platform that connects judicial entities across the country.

What I enjoy most is owning a problem end to end. I like to understand what slows a team down, design both the backend and the UI, and ship something non-technical people can use without waiting on an engineer. Some of that work has turned processes that took days into minutes.

Day to day I work with Python, TypeScript, React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, GraphQL, Temporal and LLM APIs on Google Cloud. Earlier in my career I worked with NestJS and Node.
